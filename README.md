# 🦁 Auronix learningAI — Free AI Study Notes, Formula Sheets & Practice Test Generator

**🌐 Live Web App:** [https://auronix-learning-ai.streamlit.app/](https://auronix-learning-ai.streamlit.app/)

**Auronix learningAI** is a free, instant AI-powered study companion built for **Class 11, Class 12, JEE, NEET, and Competitive Exam** students. Turn any chapter, topic, or educational YouTube lecture into topper-level notes and interactive practice tests in seconds!

---

### ✨ Key Features
* **📚 Deep Topper Exam Notes & Formulas:** Instant structured revision notes with definitions, laws, SI units, and solved numericals.
* **⚡ 60-Sec Rapid Formula & Trick Sheet:** Last-minute exam formulas, shortcuts, and common traps to avoid.
* **🔗 YouTube Lecture Summarizer:** Paste any educational YouTube lecture link to extract structured study material automatically.
* **🎯 Test Your Knowledge (Interactive Quiz Engine):** Generate custom **10, 20, or 30-question** tests (`Easy`, `Medium`, or `Hard / JEE Level`) in both **Interactive MCQ** (with live score & explanations) and **Subjective Q&A** formats.
* **📑 Smart PDF / Print Export:** Download clean, formatted study notes and tests with proper mathematical equations ready to save as PDF.
* **🌐 Bilingual Support:** Available in both **English (Exam Ready)** and **Hinglish (Easy Concept Clarity)**.

---

### 🚀 How to Use
1. Open **[Auronix learningAI](https://auronix-learning-ai.streamlit.app/)**.
2. Choose either **YouTube Lecture Link** or type any **Chapter / Topic Name**.
3. Click **Generate Notes Now** and practice with **Test Your Knowledge** below!
